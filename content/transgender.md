## Coming to be trans

### Life as an egg

I am five years old. I love Angry Birds, racing, LEGOs, monster trucks.... I'm not a 'boy'. It's not important to me. But I know what I'm allowed to enjoy, and I do enjoy these things. These things would be been classified as autistic interests. My room is adorned with Angry Birds and Minecraft posters and racing flags, and is covered in LEGOs. It's full and hardly minimalistic.

I am seven years old. My family gets a Wii U at some point, and through the game _LEGO Dimensions_ I discover the _Portal_ franchise. I start programming and playing games on my dad's laptop. Subconsciously, I become less interested in my previous interests.

I am eleven years old. My body begins to change. Without even realizing it, I very suddenly lose all interest in everything even remotely boyish; everything else continues to be an interest of mine. Mysteriously[^1], my room quickly empties out, and for years remains ultra minimal. No more posters, racing flags, or LEGOs, only bare walls, a bed, and a dresser remain. From now on, I subconsciously avoid anything masculine. I don't consider "girl stuff" an option. I tend toward what's comfortable and as unisex as possible.

I need new clothes. I hate clothes. I hate trying them on. I hate seeing them on me. It's so long, boring, unexciting....

Gender only exists outside of me. My gender doesn't matter, I never think about it. I'm just a boy, and there's nothing I can do about that, except ignore it.

### Discovery

I discovered furries when I was very young, through playing the game _Starbound_ on my dad's laptop. I really really liked anthropomorphic characters! I didn't _really_ get into furry until I had proper internet access though.

On the internet, except for a brief period of time when I initially got internet access at 13, I've always been hugely anti-social. I had Discord and Reddit, but I would only ever lurk. I never commented on YouTube videos, and it took me awhile to even press the like button (on any platform). About a year ago though, I randomly decided to change that and improve my social anxiety, so I found 1-3 small furry Discord servers that looked fun and began to interact in them. A lot happened there, but more importantly during that time I started to see a lot of furry femboy[^2] gifs. Something happened :3 I realized I actually really really like feminine clothing! Being feminine and embracing it was so, so much better than being apathetic!! I thought that I must be a femboy. I still remember consciously thinking that, "well, there's no way I'm trans," multiple times though.

As I slowly unraveled the all of the deeply layered coping mechanisms I had built up over the years in order to ignore the gender I was assigned at birth, I began to realize that this _probably_ went a bit deeper than just being a femboy. At some point, I tried using she/her pronouns with a couple of my closest online friends, briefly considered the possibility that i was "just a femboy that likes fem terms and going by she/her,"[^3] before realizing that actually... i'm just a girl.

i'm not a boy at all! :3333

### my headspace post-discovery

I've been sosososo excited to finally really know that I'm a girl :3 I have so many things to look forward to, so many things I've missed out on, so many outfits to try, and there's so much I need to do!!!!

A lot of my time thinking about myself and my self-expression (which I spend a lot more time thinking about now, since I don't have to avoid thinking about it anymore) has been spent thinking about what I _will_ do and what I _will_ wear, and just generally getting excited and delerious over happy thoughts over the future. I unfortunately don't live in the most supportive household of all time, but i'm ultimately optimistic about the future :3

I also am very excited about the idea of one day being able to begin HRT. It's really validating to research [the effects of HRT](https://genderdysphoria.fyi/en/second-puberty-fem) and get excited over every single one, whether positive (soft skin! less body hair!! fat redistribution!!!) or even negative (period-like symptoms for some reason!! obviously not as bad as an afab person's period, but this still happens apparently!!). Even for the few negative effects, they're negatively _feminine_, so i'm oddly excited for them too :P

#### dysphoria

There's downsides to knowing i'm a girl too, of course. I never really got "dysphoric" before, as I did everything I could growing up to just _avoid_ the issue, whether I consciously realized it or not. Since I know now, I get dysphoric all the time... which isn't fun TwT
I avoided mirrors before (without realizing it or knowing why), but now I can't stand seeing myself looking all masculine in the mirror. Usually the first thing I notice is all my facial/body hair, and usually I instantly grab my electric razor and buzz it off.
I've also noticed that if a shirt feels too short or small when I try it on looking for something to wear, I'll instantly move on to try something a bit larger. Before knowing, I was mostly fine if a shirt was a bit small, as long as it was comfortable I would just put on whatever I grabbed first. But now if a shirt makes me feel masculine that also makes me uncomfortable :P I bet if I had more outfits I actually wanted to wear, I'd also probably spend more time caring about what I wear each day.
